# Seedy Research

Seedy Research is a Thai-first research context that connects locally published knowledge to the global research conversation without erasing provenance, access rights, or the boundary between evidence and discovery metadata.

## Product language

**Seedy Research**:
The researcher-facing product for discovering, reading, connecting, and advancing Thai research.
_Avoid_: CivilMCP, SeedyMCP when referring to the whole user-facing product

**SeedyMCP**:
The human-agent collaboration layer through which a research agent and a person work on the same visible research state.
_Avoid_: CivilMCP, generic AI wrapper

**Thai Research Graph**:
The canonical network of Thai works, source records, full-paper assets, people, institutions, versions, and scholarly relationships, with provider provenance preserved.
_Avoid_: paper database, scraped corpus

**Civil Research Pack**:
The first subject collection and proof vertical within Seedy Research, focused on Thai civil-engineering evidence.
_Avoid_: CivilMCP, the whole platform

**Primary Researcher**:
A Thai researcher or Thai–global research team seeking to turn under-indexed local evidence into a visible, reusable, and testable contribution to global research.
_Avoid_: every paper reader, a university administrator as the primary user

**Research Impact**:
Observable reuse of Thai research in a reviewed synthesis, global comparison, next-study protocol, collaboration lead, or attributed research decision.
_Avoid_: visibility alone, inferred citation impact, unsupported commercialization claims

**Evidence-to-Impact Bridge**:
The product position in which overlooked Thai evidence is carried through human verification into a reusable global connection or next-study decision.
_Avoid_: Thailand's alphaXiv as the primary product claim, generic AI research copilot

**Institutional Buyer**:
A university, library, research office, or research institute that funds governed repository onboarding, coverage intelligence, private collections, and institutional integrations while the researcher-facing core remains broadly accessible.
_Avoid_: the individual researcher as the initial primary payer

## Research journey

**Thai-to-Global Research Path**:
A bounded journey from Thai research discovery through source review, literature synthesis, global connection mapping, a candidate gap, and a next-study protocol; this literature-to-study-design outcome is the primary end-to-end promise.
_Avoid_: end-to-end research when implying that data collection, analysis, manuscript writing, or publication has already been completed

**Research Passport**:
A portable, human-reviewed artifact that carries selected Thai evidence, its source anchors, bounded global connections, and unresolved validation needs.
_Avoid_: peer-review report, proof of novelty

**Next-Study Protocol**:
A proposed research question, data or method, and next validation step derived from the reviewed Research Path.
_Avoid_: completed study, validated experiment

**Manuscript Support**:
A secondary workflow that helps organize reviewed evidence, systematic-review scaffolding, and bounded draft components without claiming that a comprehensive review or completed manuscript exists.
_Avoid_: autonomous paper writing, generated scientific conclusions

**Research Notebook**:
A source-scoped workspace for public papers and private uploads in which a researcher can run agentic search, inspect exact-page citations, keep notes and evidence matrices, and promote reviewed findings into a Research Passport and Research Path.
_Avoid_: generic chat with PDFs, autonomous manuscript factory

**OpenRAG Adapter**:
An optional integration boundary that lets a Research Notebook use OpenRAG-compatible ingestion or agentic retrieval while Seedy Research retains canonical identity, rights, access, and evidence-promotion authority.
_Avoid_: replacing the Seedy Research system of record, treating OpenRAG citations as automatically citable evidence

## Evidence boundaries

**Page-Linked Evidence**:
Source content tied to a stable page anchor that can support a claim after a person reopens and reviews it.
_Avoid_: search result, abstract-only match

**Global Research Lead**:
An external bibliographic record or scholarly relationship used for discovery and comparison but not yet admitted as evidence.
_Avoid_: global evidence, verified citation

**Candidate Gap**:
An unresolved method, context, population, outcome, or validation question suggested by the reviewed literature.
_Avoid_: proven novelty, validated research gap

**Full-Paper Access State**:
The declared lawful way a research asset may be read, ranging from verified native reading to source-hosted, restricted, metadata-only, or unavailable access.
_Avoid_: full text available without a rights decision

**National Discovery Coverage**:
Measured metadata visibility across named Thai research providers, reported with a provider denominator, freshness, and unresolved coverage gaps.
_Avoid_: complete Thai research database, full-paper coverage

**Coverage Expansion**:
Provider-by-provider growth of canonical metadata and lawful full-paper assets, with discovery and evidence coverage measured separately.
_Avoid_: scrape everything, record count as proof of quality

**Evidence Coverage**:
The subset of discovered research that has passed rights, provenance, identity, page-mapping, and quality gates and may support bounded claims.
_Avoid_: searchable records, metadata coverage

**Native Full-Paper Coverage**:
The number and proportion of discovered works whose specific assets are authorized, integrity-checked, page-mapped, and readable inside Seedy Research.
_Avoid_: publicly reachable PDF count, metadata records with outbound full-text links

**Public Native Corpus**:
The set of rights-cleared full-paper assets that Seedy Research may serve natively to the users authorized by each asset policy and count in public native coverage.
_Avoid_: private uploads, source-hosted links

**Private Workspace Corpus**:
Full-paper assets supplied by a person or institution for a bounded private workspace; they do not enter the public graph, public coverage count, model training, or public evidence corpus without a separate promotion decision.
_Avoid_: public native corpus, automatic institutional publication

**Institutional Deposit**:
A governed metadata or full-paper delivery from a university or research institute through an approved batch, OAI, API, or repository feed with provenance, rights, integrity, and takedown ownership.
_Avoid_: unrestricted institutional crawling, permission inferred from reachability

**Repository Visibility & Verified Reader Pilot**:
The initial institutional offer combining a repository visibility audit, a rights-governed full-paper deposit, and dated coverage intelligence without transferring copyright or enabling model training by default.
_Avoid_: national repository replacement, uncontrolled institutional mirror, unvalidated subscription product

**Reproducible Impact Benchmark**:
A public scenario that another person can replay from missed or under-indexed Thai discovery through exact-page review, global connection, and a bounded research outcome.
_Avoid_: an unconsented pilot testimonial, a private result presented as public validation
